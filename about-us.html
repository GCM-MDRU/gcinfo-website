About GAYATRI COMMUNICATIONS

Local connectivity and service support for our community.

Connecting homes and businesses

GAYATRI COMMUNICATIONS is a local business based in Meduru Village, Gampalagudem Mandal, NTR District, Andhra Pradesh, India.

We provide internet and broadband connectivity, cable television services and selected digital and assisted services, subject to service availability, technical feasibility and applicable provider terms.

Our goal is to give customers clear information about available services, plans and charges, with a local point of contact for enquiries and assistance.

Our services

  • Fiber internet and broadband plans
  • Cable television plans and available channel options
  • Customer assistance for service and billing enquiries
  • Online account and payment access through our customer portal

Service availability

Coverage and installation depend on network availability at the customer’s address. Please contact us to confirm available plans, charges, installation requirements and service terms before ordering.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 01 September 2026

GAYATRI COMMUNICATIONS provides Internet and broadband services and related online payment/recharge facilities through its customer portal.

1. Recharge / Payment Cancellation

Once an online recharge or payment has been successfully completed, the transaction is generally final and cannot be cancelled or reversed by the customer. Customers are responsible for entering correct account details and payment amounts before completing a transaction.

2. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered where an amount is debited but the corresponding recharge/service payment is not received; a duplicate payment is received for the same transaction; or a payment cannot be applied to the customer account due to a technical issue. Eligibility is determined after transaction verification.

3. Refund Request

Email gcinfo009@gmail.com and provide customer name, customer account/username, transaction amount and date, order/transaction reference, registered mobile number, and payment confirmation or transaction details.

4. Refund Processing

After verification and approval, eligible refunds will generally be processed within 7 working days. The time for funds to appear depends on the bank, card or payment service provider.

5. Non-Refundable Transactions

Refunds will generally not be provided for successfully completed and correctly applied recharges, services already consumed, incorrect customer/account details provided by the customer, failure to use an active service, or requests after successful service delivery, except where required by applicable law.

6. Service Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of Internet/broadband service by contacting us. Cancellation is subject to applicable service terms and outstanding dues.

7. Partial Refunds

Where applicable, partial refunds will be determined based on the unused portion of the service, applicable terms and the circumstances of cancellation.

8. Force Majeure

Refund or service commitments may be affected by circumstances beyond our reasonable control, including natural disasters, network failures, government restrictions, power failures or other force majeure events.

9. Governing Law

This policy is governed by applicable laws of India.

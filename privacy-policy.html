We may collect your name, mobile number, email address, service/customer account details, installation and service address, payment and transaction details, login and account information, customer-support communications, and technical information such as IP address, browser type and device information.

Payments made through our customer portal may be processed through third-party payment gateway providers. We do not store complete credit card, debit card or banking credentials on our own servers. Payment information is processed by the applicable payment service provider.

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers, payment gateway providers, technology providers, network/service partners, and government or regulatory authorities where required to provide services, process transactions or comply with applicable law.

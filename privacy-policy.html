Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

GAYATRI COMMUNICATIONS respects customer privacy and handles personal information for legitimate business purposes related to service enquiries, customer account administration, billing, payments and customer support.

Information we may collect

Depending on the service used, we may collect a customer's name, contact details, service address, account or customer reference, service selections, payment status and communications made to customer support.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, provision and manage services, maintain billing records, reconcile payments, assist customers, prevent misuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Payments and service providers

Payments may be processed by third-party banks and payment gateway providers. Payment processing is subject to the relevant provider's terms and privacy practices. We may share information with service providers where necessary to provide the requested service or reconcile a transaction.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information and retain it for as long as needed for service delivery, business records and legal requirements. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Customer requests

For privacy questions or requests relating to your information, contact us using the verified details on our Contact Us page.
